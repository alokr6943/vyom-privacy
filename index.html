Vyom Browser Privacy Policy

App: Vyom Browser (com.vyom.browser) · Developer: AR Innovation · Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short: Vyom has no account, no analytics, no ads of its own and no servers. Your history, tabs and settings stay on your phone. Web pages come straight from the sites you visit, and searches go to the search engine you choose.

1. What stays on your phone

Vyom stores your browsing history, open tabs, home shortcuts, search engine and other settings, the per-site Shield switches and your download list only on your device, in the app's private storage. Normal tabs keep cookies and site data like any browser. Private tabs don't save history. Where your phone's Android System WebView supports separate profiles (current versions do), private tabs also keep their cookies and site data in a separate profile that's deleted when you close the last private tab. Android cloud backup is turned off for Vyom. The developer never receives any of this data.

2. Websites and search engines

When you open a page, your phone connects directly to that website, which receives the usual information any browser sends (such as your IP address and the page requested). When you search, your search words go to the search engine you chose (Google by default, or Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo, Ecosia, Brave or a custom one). Those websites and search engines handle that data under their own privacy policies. Vyom doesn't send it anywhere else.

Search suggestions in the address bar are built on your phone from your own history and the text you type. Vyom doesn't fetch suggestions from a server.

3. Vyom Shield

Shield blocks requests to known ad and tracker domains using a list built into the app. The checking happens on your phone, and the blocked counts are only shown to you.

4. Downloads

Files you download come directly from the server you chose and are saved to Downloads/Vyom on your phone. A notification shows download progress while files are being saved.

5. Permissions

PermissionWhy
CameraOnly for the QR scanner, when you open it. Codes are read on your phone using on-device Google ML Kit, and the camera images aren't saved or sent anywhere. ML Kit's usage reporting is turned off.
Internet / network stateTo load web pages and downloads, and to resume downloads when the connection returns.
Notifications and foreground serviceTo show download progress and keep downloads running while you use other apps.
Voice searchWhen you tap the microphone, Vyom opens your phone's speech recognition app. That app handles your voice and returns only the text.

Websites may ask for access to your camera, microphone or location. Vyom currently denies these requests.

6. Sharing

Vyom doesn't sell or share your data. When you use Share, the link goes only to the app you choose.

7. Deleting your data

You can clear history in the app, close tabs, or uninstall Vyom to delete everything it stored. The developer holds no copy of your data.

8. Children

Vyom is a general web browser and isn't designed for children under 13.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
